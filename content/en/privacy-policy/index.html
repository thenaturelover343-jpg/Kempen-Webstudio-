Who is responsible?

The party responsible for the personal data that come in through this website is Webstudio K, Kemelbeekstraat 16, 2460 Kasterlee.

For any question about your data, email info@kempenwebstudio.be.

Which data do we ask for in the contact form?

In the contact form you enter your name, the name of your business (not required), your email address, the service your question is about and your message.

Your request through the form is forwarded to us via an external form service and so arrives in our mailbox. We do not keep it longer than needed.

If the form cannot send your request directly, your own email programme opens with your request already filled in. You then decide yourself whether to send that email. If you email or call us directly, we only receive the data you give us yourself.

In our sample designs you can try out planners and forms. What you enter there is not sent and is not stored.

What do we use your data for?

  • To answer your question.
  • To prepare a no-obligation quote for your website and discuss it with you, if you ask for one.
  • To send you a quote and, if you become a client, to carry out the agreed assignment.

We use your data on the basis of your own request, in preparation for a possible contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), and on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering questions (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not send you a newsletter and we do not sell or rent out your data.

How long do we keep your data?

We keep your request, your email and your data for a maximum of 12 months after our last contact. After that we delete them. If you become a client, we keep your data for as long as the assignment runs, and afterwards only what the law requires us to keep for the accounts.

Who can see your data?

Only Webstudio K reads your messages. This website is hosted with an external hosting partner, the contact form is forwarded to us through an external form service and our email runs through an external email service. They process your data only in order to provide those services. They may also process data outside the European Union, with appropriate safeguards such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission.

Cookies

This website only stores in your browser which cookie choice you made, so that we do not ask again on every page. If you give consent for statistics, statistics help us understand how the website is used, so we can improve it. For this we work with an external provider, without cookies that track you. We do not use cookies for marketing.

You can read more in our cookie policy. You can always change your choice via at the bottom of every page.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have them corrected or to have them erased. You can also ask for the use to be restricted, object to the use, or request your data in a commonly used format. To do so, email info@kempenwebstudio.be. We reply within one month.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, you can lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Authority, Drukpersstraat 35, 1000 Brussel, www.gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be.

Security

This website works only over a secure connection (https).

Changes

If we update this privacy policy, the new version is always on this page, with the date of that version. This version is dated 29 October 2026.

A question about your data?

Email info@kempenwebstudio.be. We reply personally, within one month.
